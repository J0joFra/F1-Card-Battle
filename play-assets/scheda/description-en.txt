SHORT DESCRIPTION
Collect 1,500 racing legends and beat the CPU and your friends, round by round!

FULL DESCRIPTION
From the cars of the 1950s to today's champions: in Driver Clash every driver in Grand Prix history becomes a card to collect and take to the track.

🏎️ OVER 1,500 CARDS TO COLLECT
Every driver has a card for each team they raced for, with their real stats: Grands Prix started, top 10 finishes, points per race, best finish, average grid, races finished and head-to-heads won against their teammate.
Cards come in seven rarities: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Legendary, plus the vintage Pioneer and Classic cards for the drivers of the early decades.

⭐ UNLOCK CAREER CARDS
Complete all the team cards of a driver to earn their Legendary Career card, with the numbers of their whole career.

⚔️ PICK THE STAT, WIN THE ROUND
Matches are quick and tactical: each round, one card comes from your deck and one from your opponent's. Pick the stat where you're strongest; whoever wins the round picks the next one. Each stat can only be used once, so plan ahead!

🃏 BUILD YOUR 3 DECKS
Put together three different decks and find the right balance: a deck full of stars isn't enough, you need strong cards in every stat. Each card's radar chart shows its strengths and weaknesses at a glance.

🎁 OPEN PACKS
Win matches to earn packs and credits. Choose between the Classic pack, the Premium pack with Gold cards or better, and the team pack of the day. The shop has new cards to buy every day, and Jokers let you pick the card you're missing.

👥 CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS
Invite a friend with a code and play in real time, best of 3 matches, one for each deck. Add them as a friend and start a new challenge whenever you like.

📅 SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• Themed daily challenge (a team or a decade) with a reward and a win streak
• Three daily missions that pay credits
• Rarity, decade and nationality modes
• 50 Google Play Games achievements to unlock

✨ AND MORE
• Custom avatar and favorite driver
• Your collection is saved online: with your username and access code you'll find it on any phone
• Light and dark theme, seasonal events
• Available in English, Italian, French, Spanish and German
• No ads and no in-app purchases: everything is unlocked by playing

Driver Clash is an unofficial fan-made game and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Formula 1, the FIA or any team. Driver names and statistics are used for informational purposes only.
